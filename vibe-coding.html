Spotify • Wellness • Productivity

emos (tune your work self) Spotify Playlists for Work's Emotional Damage

Impact
100% No-scroll compliance across mobile and desktop No-scroll compliance (yes, the whole thing fits on one screen)

A web app that recommends Spotify playlists based on your work role and emotional state. Features an emotion wheel, plus toggles for instrumental music and a language selector. Running PostHog analytics for session replays to observe user interactions and iterating on the UI for maximum engagement and vibe.

Ever been in a standup so draining you needed a whole playlist to recover? EMOS has a 3-layer emotion wheel so you can pinpoint exactly how dead inside you feel, then serves you the perfect Spotify playlist. Instrumental toggle for when words are too much. Language selector for when English isn't cutting it either.
