Core Product Philosophy My Actual Operating System

01

Reliability First Plan for the Chaos

Production AI needs to fail gracefully. I design for the edge cases, building products that degrade predictably rather than catastrophically when things go wrong.

Your AI will hallucinate. Your pipeline will break at 3 AM. I build for the moment everything goes wrong, because it will. The only question is whether you planned for it.

02

Human-in-the-Loop Robots Need Adult Supervision

AI augments human judgment, doesn't replace it. I always design for oversight — ensuring humans stay in control of critical decisions while AI handles the scale.

Every PM who says "let the AI decide" has never explained to a VP why the model auto-approved $2M in spend. I build guardrails first, autonomy second.

03

Measurable Outcomes Vibes Are Not Metrics

Every AI feature needs clear success metrics tied to business value. I focus on defining measurable outcomes before building, connecting model performance to user impact.

"The AI feels smarter" is not a KPI. I make sure every feature has a number attached to it that someone on the business side actually cares about.

04

Progressive Trust Earn It, Don't Demand It

Build user confidence through transparency and predictable behavior. I design AI products that earn trust incrementally — starting conservative, then expanding as users gain comfort.

Nobody trusts an AI that launches with "we'll handle everything." I ship the version that says "here's what I did and why" and let users turn up the dial themselves.
