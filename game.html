🎮 Basic Rules

  • •Use arrow keys (↑ ↓ ← →) or swipe to slide all tiles
  • •When two tiles with the same item touch, they merge into one
  • •Each merge creates the next item in the progression
  • •After every move, a new tile appears randomly
  • •Your goal is to run a Launch (2048)! 🌟
